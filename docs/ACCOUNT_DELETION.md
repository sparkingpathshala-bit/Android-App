# Account and Data Deletion Policy — Sparking Pathshala

**Effective Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Website:** [https://sparkingpathshala.in/delete-account](https://sparkingpathshala.in/delete-account)  
**App:** Sparking Pathshala (`com.sparkingpathshala.app`)

---

## 1. Google Play & DPDP Compliance Declaration
Google Play Store policy mandates that apps allowing users to create accounts must also provide an in-app path and an accessible web link for users to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

In accordance with Section 12 of India’s **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)** and Google Play Data Safety policies, Sparking Pathshala guarantees every student and legal guardian the **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")**.

---

## 2. How to Delete Your Account

### Option A: Direct In-App Deletion (Instant & Automated)
1. Open the **Sparking Pathshala** application.
2. Navigate to the **Profile** tab from the bottom navigation bar.
3. Scroll to the **Danger Zone** at the bottom of the screen.
4. Tap **"Delete Account"**.
5. Read the confirmation dialog warning:
   > *"Warning: Deleting your account will permanently remove your student records, fees status, test scores, attendance, and doubt tickets. This action cannot be undone."*
6. Tap **"Delete permanently"**.
7. The app invalidates your session token, wipes local encrypted storage, and sends an authenticated deletion request to `api.sparkingpathshala.in`.

---

### Option B: Web Request (Without Installing the App)
If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device:
1. Visit our web deletion portal: [https://sparkingpathshala.in/delete-account](https://sparkingpathshala.in/delete-account)
2. Submit your registered Student Email Address and Contact Number.
3. An OTP verification code will be sent to confirm identity.
4. Once verified, your deletion ticket is automatically processed.

Alternatively, you can email our Grievance Officer directly:
- **Email:** [support@sparkingpathshala.in](mailto:support@sparkingpathshala.in) with subject: `Account Deletion Request - [Your Registered Email]`
- **Processing Time:** Confirmation sent within 48 hours; complete data purge executed within 7 business days.

---

## 3. What Data Is Deleted Permanently
Upon account deletion, the following data is permanently purged from our active production database:
- Student Name, Email, Phone Number, and Class details.
- All Quiz submissions, answers, scores, and percentile history.
- Homework files and assignment upload history.
- On-Demand Doubt tickets and chat logs.
- Attendance registers and notification records.

---

## 4. What Data Is Retained (Legal Exceptions)
In compliance with Indian taxation laws (Goods and Services Tax Act) and accounting regulations, historical **fee transaction invoice numbers and GST payment receipts** are archived in anonymized, read-only accounting archives for the legally mandated statutory audit period (up to 7 years), after which they are permanently shredded. No academic telemetry or personal profiling data is retained.
