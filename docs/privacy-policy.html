Sparking Pathshala

Official Privacy Policy & Data Protection Charter

Effective Date: November 14, 2026 | App Package: com.sparkingpathshala.app

Compliance Guarantee: This Privacy Policy strictly complies with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the Google Play Store Families & Data Safety Policies.

1. Commitment to Student Privacy

Sparking Pathshala ("we", "our", or "us") provides structured academic coaching, practice quizzes, digital library resources, homework tracking, and attendance management for students from Classes 1 to 12. We operate under strict principles of data minimization and educational purpose limitation.

2. Data We Collect

A. Student & Guardian Information

  • Identity & Contact: Student Name, Email Address, Contact Number, Enrolled Academic Class (Class 1 to 12).
  • Academic Performance: Quiz responses, test scores, attendance logs, and uploaded homework sheets.
  • Tuition Receipts: Transaction reference IDs, invoice dates, and fee clearance status. (Raw card numbers or UPI MPINs are never accessed or stored).

B. Technical Diagnostics

  • Device model, Android OS version, and crash diagnostic logs to maintain stability on diverse devices (₹10,000 budget phones to high-end devices).

3. Zero Advertising & No Data Selling Policy

Sparking Pathshala contains ZERO advertisements, does NOT collect the Google Advertising ID (AAID), and will NEVER sell or monetize student data to advertisers, third-party marketing brokers, or commercial entities.

4. Child & Minor Protection (DPDP Section 9)

For students under 18 years of age, registration implies verifiable authorization from a parent or legal guardian. We strictly prohibit behavioral profiling or targeted algorithms aimed at children. Doubt forums are actively moderated by faculty.

5. Bank-Grade Security & Encryption

  • In Transit: Encrypted using TLS 1.3 with Certificate Pinning against api.sparkingpathshala.in.
  • At Rest: Stored using AES-256 GCM encryption backed by the hardware Android Keystore.

6. Right to Erasure & Account Deletion

In accordance with Google Play mandates and DPDP Section 12, users can delete their account at any time:

  • In-App: Open Sparking Pathshala ➔ Profile ➔ Delete Account permanently.
  • On the Web: Visit our dedicated deletion portal at https://sparkingpathshala.in/delete-account.
