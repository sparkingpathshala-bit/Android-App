## 1. Introduction & Regulatory Commitment
Sparking Pathshala ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to empowering students from Classes 1 to 12 through holistic academic coaching, practice quizzes, digital library resources, homework tracking, and attendance management.

We recognize the immense responsibility of handling student data and strictly abide by:
- **India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)** and rules framed thereunder.
- **The Information Technology Act, 2000** and Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.
- **Google Play Store Developer Policies**, specifically the **Families Policy**, **Children’s Online Privacy**, and **Data Safety Standards**.

---

## 2. Information We Collect
We strictly enforce a policy of **Data Minimization**. We only collect the minimal academic and account information necessary to administer educational coaching.

### A. Information You Provide
1. **Student & Guardian Profile Data:** Name, email address, mobile phone number, academic grade/standard (Class 1 to Class 12), and enrolled subjects.
2. **Academic Work & Telemetry:** Quiz attempts, scores, time taken per question, submitted homework sheets, attendance records, and doubt clearing queries.
3. **Tuition & Billing Data:** Digital transaction references, payment dates, fee status, and receipts. *Note: We never collect, process, or store raw credit/debit card numbers or UPI MPINs. All digital transactions are handled via RBI-authorized payment aggregators.*

### B. Device & Technical Data
1. **Device Specifications:** Device model, operating system version (Android release & API level), and app version code for bug diagnosis and crash resolution.
2. **Local Cache Storage:** Downloaded study notes and question banks cached locally on your device for offline reading.

### C. Explicit Declarations on Non-Collection
- **NO Advertising ID (AAID/GAID):** We do NOT collect, read, or transmit advertising identifiers.
- **NO Location Tracking:** We do NOT collect fine or background GPS coordinates.
- **NO Biometric Data:** We do NOT record fingerprints or facial recognition telemetry.
- **NO Third-Party Trackers or Data Brokers:** We do NOT share or sell student records to any data brokers, ad networks, or social media platforms.

---

## 3. Purpose of Processing (Why We Use Your Data)
Under Section 4 and Section 7 of the DPDP Act, personal data is processed strictly for legitimate educational uses:
1. **Enabling Core Coaching Services:** Generating customized quizzes, tracking chapter progression, syllabus coverage, and homework completion.
2. **Academic Performance Analytics:** Calculating attendance percentages and test averages to provide actionable feedback to students and parents.
3. **Fee & Receipt Management:** Providing transparent accounting ledgers and digital fee receipts.
4. **App Reliability & Safety:** Preventing brute-force attacks, tamper detection, and crash diagnostics via encrypted error reporting.

---

## 4. Special Protections for Children & Minors (DPDP Act Section 9)
Because our users include minors under 18 years of age (Classes 1–12):
1. **Verifiable Parental Consent:** Enrollment of a minor implies consent from a parent or legal guardian.
2. **Prohibition of Behavioral Tracking:** We strictly prohibit behavioral monitoring, targeted advertising, or algorithmic profiling aimed at children.
3. **Safe Environment:** The doubt forum and student channels are moderated by verified faculty. Direct unfiltered peer-to-peer messaging is disabled to prevent cyber-bullying.

---

## 5. Data Security & Storage Architecture
We implement enterprise-grade technical and organizational safeguards:
- **In-Transit Encryption:** All communication between the app and `api.sparkingpathshala.in` is strictly encrypted using **TLS 1.3** and protected by **Certificate Pinning**.
- **At-Rest Encryption:** Local user preferences, tokens, and cached notes are encrypted with **AES-256 GCM** utilizing the hardware-backed **Android Keystore System**.
- **Server Infrastructure:** Production databases operate in secure, ISO 27001 / SOC 2 certified data centers located within Indian jurisdiction.

---

## 6. Data Principal Rights (Your Rights Under DPDP Act)
Students and legal guardians hold full authority over their personal data:
1. **Right to Access & Review:** You can view all enrolled classes, scores, attendance, and fee history directly within the app.
2. **Right to Correction:** You can update personal details or class levels from the Profile screen or by emailing support.
3. **Right to Erasure & Account Deletion:**
   - **In-App:** Profile Screen ➔ Danger Zone ➔ **Delete Account permanently**.
   - **Web Portal:** Accessible at [https://sparkingpathshala.in/delete-account](https://sparkingpathshala.in/delete-account).
   Upon confirmation, all student test scores, homework, and profile records are permanently purged from active databases within 7 working days.
4. **Right to Grievance Redressal:** You have the right to register complaints regarding your privacy rights.

---

## 7. Data Retention Policy
- Active student records are maintained for the duration of the coaching enrollment plus 1 academic year for record-keeping and tax compliance (fees receipts).
- Temporary crash dumps and telemetry logs are automatically truncated after 30 days.
